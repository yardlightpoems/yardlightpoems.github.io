Privacy Policy

Yardlight Publisher · Last updated October 18, 2026

This policy explains how Yardlight Publisher (the "App") handles information. The App is a private publishing tool operated by Yardlight. Its only purpose is to upload and schedule videos created by Yardlight to accounts owned by Yardlight: our YouTube channel (@yardlightpoems), our Facebook page and our Instagram account (@yardlight_).

Who uses the App

The App is used only by the Yardlight account owner. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and is not used to access anyone else's account or data.

What data the App accesses

YouTube (YouTube API Services). With the owner's authorization, the App uses the scopes youtube.upload (to upload and schedule our own videos, with their title, description and tags) and youtube.readonly (to read our own channel's basic information and confirm that each upload was published correctly).

Facebook and Instagram (Meta APIs). The App publishes our own videos to our own Facebook page and Instagram account, reads their publication status and basic statistics, and helps us reply to comments on our own posts.

The App does not access, collect or store personal data of viewers, subscribers or other users, and it does not access any account other than Yardlight's.

How data is stored and protected

Authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in files readable only by that user account. Videos and captions are files we create ourselves. No data obtained through the APIs is sold, rented, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling.

Retention and deletion

Authorization tokens are kept only while the App is in use and are deleted when it is no longer needed. Publication records (video ID, date and status) are kept for our own publishing log. You can revoke the App's access to a Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, and to a Meta account in the Facebook settings under "Business integrations".

Third-party services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using YouTube content you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Meta's handling of data is described in the Meta Privacy Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
