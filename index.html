A new poem every day

Yardlight publishes a short, original poem every day — words for the people who held everyone together and were rarely thanked for it. Each poem is read aloud over a quiet painted scene and shared as a short video.

Yardlight Publisher is a small private tool used only by the Yardlight team to upload and schedule our own videos on our own Facebook page, Instagram account and YouTube channel. It does not offer any service to the public and does not collect data from viewers.
